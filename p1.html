1. Executive Summary

The data reveals a significant shift in the timing and severity of heatwaves between 2025 and 2026. While 2025 saw a higher total number of heat-related illnesses, 2026 was markedly more lethal, recording a higher number of fatalities despite having fewer total reported cases.

4. Key Findings

Severity of 2026

Although 2026 had roughly 10% fewer reported illnesses (3,219 vs. 3,589), the death toll rose by 41% (31 vs. 22). This suggests that the heat events in 2026 may have been more intense or affected more vulnerable populations.

Shift in Peak Heat

In 2025, the heat crisis was concentrated in early-to-mid July. In 2026, the peak shifted to early August, specifically the first week of August (Aug 3–8), which saw a sustained period of high illness and fatality rates.

Lethality of August 2026

The period between August 3 and August 8, 2026, was particularly devastating, recording 14 deaths in just six days.
